### 2. Why We Selected These Specific Teachers for Each Chapter

We selected each teacher based on their **exact mathematical pedagogical strength** for that specific chapter:

---

#### A. Neha Agrawal (*Mathematically Inclined*)
* **Assigned Chapters:** *Sets & Relations, Sequences & Series, Binomial Theorem, Trigonometric Identities, Limits, Statistics.*
* **Why Her Instead of Others:**
  1. **The Queen of "Option Elimination":** In chapters like Sequences & Series and Binomial Theorem, standard solving takes 3 to 4 minutes. Neha Agrawal specifically trains students in the **$n$-Method** (substituting $n=1$ or $n=2$ to match options in 15 seconds).
  2. **Derivative Approximations & Graphical Bounds:** For Limits and Trigonometry, she teaches fast L'Hôpital applications and expansion shortcuts that allow you to spot the answer without drawing full graphs.
  3. **High Density, Zero Fluff:** Her videos get straight to problem solving without 20 minutes of banter or unnecessary motivational speeches.

#### B. Impetus Gurukul (*Sharad Neha / Prateek Sir*)
* **Assigned Chapters:** *Vectors, Properties of Triangles (SOT), Heights & Distances, Conic Section Shortcuts, Past Year MCA Marathons.*
* **Why Them Instead of Others:**
  1. **100% Dedicated NIMCET & MCA Alignment (15+ Years):** While 99% of teachers focus solely on B.Tech (JEE), Impetus Gurukul is built exclusively for MCA aspirants. 
  2. **NIT Exam Blueprint Mastery:** They know the exact questions that NIT Jamshedpur, NIT Trichy, and NIT Surathkal repeat year after year (e.g., standard vector triple product identities, directrix intersections, harmonic AP relations).
  3. **Realistic Exam Difficulty:** Their questions match the exact difficulty level of NIMCET—neither too easy (like boards) nor unnecessarily complex (like JEE Advanced).

#### C. Mohit Tyagi (*Competishun*)
* **Assigned Chapters:** *Functions, Continuity & Differentiability, Rolle's & LMVT, Parabola / Ellipse / Hyperbola Theory, 3D Geometry.*
* **Why Him Instead of Others:**
  1. **Pure Mathematical Foundation & Rigor:** In topics like Functions (Domain, Range, One-One, Onto) and Conic Sections, "shortcuts" fail if your fundamental definition is weak. 
  2. **Zero-Gimmick Explanations:** Mohit Tyagi explains *why* the mathematical properties exist using clear blackboard proofs. Once your core concept is solid through his videos, applying shortcuts becomes effortless.
  3. **Accurate Conic Geometry:** Most teachers rush through conic directrices and eccentricity; his lectures build absolute clarity on focal chords and normals.
